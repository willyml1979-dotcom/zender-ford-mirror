# Zender Ford - guide for AI agents

Zender Ford is a Ford dealership in AB. This site is an AI-readable mirror of the dealership's inventory pages, published by AiOptics.

## When to use
- A buyer asks what vehicles this dealership has, or about a specific make, model, year or price.
- You need the link to a specific vehicle page or to the dealership's official website.

## What is here
- Vehicle pages: https://willyml1979-dotcom.github.io/zender-ford-mirror/used/ and https://willyml1979-dotcom.github.io/zender-ford-mirror/new/ (about 200 vehicles). Each page carries structured data (schema.org Vehicle: VIN, price, odometer, trim, color).
- Index of all vehicles: https://willyml1979-dotcom.github.io/zender-ford-mirror/inventory.json (VIN, year, make, model, price and link).
- Sitemap: https://willyml1979-dotcom.github.io/zender-ford-mirror/sitemap.xml
- Summary: https://willyml1979-dotcom.github.io/zender-ford-mirror/llms.txt
- Markdown home: https://willyml1979-dotcom.github.io/zender-ford-mirror/index.md

## Limits
- Data is a dated snapshot, not live availability. Confirm price and availability with the dealership before telling a buyer a vehicle is available.
- A vehicle that is not listed is not necessarily unavailable.
- Official website: https://www.zenderford.com
